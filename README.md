# Energy Data Analysis & Visualization

## Project Overview

This project analyzes U.S. energy generation and demand data using Tableau to explore regional energy production, renewable energy usage, power sources, and changes in electricity generation over time.

The analysis uses energy data organized by balancing authority, region, date, hour, and energy source. Interactive Tableau visualizations were created to make it easier to compare energy generation patterns across regions and power sources.

## Analysis

The Tableau workbook includes analyses of:

- **Energy Source by Region** – Compares energy generation sources across geographic regions.
- **Renewable Energy** – Examines renewable energy generation and its contribution to energy production.
- **Net Production** – Analyzes net electricity generation.
- **Hourly Difference in Generation** – Examines changes in energy generation between hours.
- **Supply and Demand by Region Over 24 Hours** – Compares regional electricity supply and demand throughout the day.
- **Utility Power Source Breakdown** – Breaks down electricity generation by individual energy sources.

## Dataset

The project uses hourly energy data containing information such as:

- Balancing Authority
- Region
- Date and Local Time
- Electricity Demand
- Net Generation
- Coal
- Natural Gas
- Nuclear
- Petroleum
- Hydropower and Pumped Storage
- Solar
- Wind
- Other Fuel Sources

A second long-format dataset organizes energy generation by individual energy source and reports generation in megawatts (MW).
